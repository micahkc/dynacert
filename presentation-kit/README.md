# dynacert slide kit (2026-11-02)

Everything needed to build a PowerPoint about dynacert, for someone who does not need to know the
tool. No technical background is assumed.

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| [SLIDES.md](SLIDES.md) | Slide-by-slide guide: headline, image, on-slide text, speaker notes |
| [images/](images/) | Figures, ready to paste. Files `01`–`09` and `16` are 16:9 and fill a slide |
| [source/](source/) | Scripts that regenerate those figures. You do not need these |

**Accuracy.** Numbers come from real runs in the source repository on 2026-11-02. Please check with
the author before changing any number or the wording of a guarantee.

**Building the deck**
- Use the headline in SLIDES.md as the slide title. The images deliberately have no titles of
  their own, except `10`–`15`, which come straight from the tool.
- Images `01`–`09` and `16` have an off-white background (`#fcfcfb`). Use a white or very light slide.
- Colors in `01`–`05`: blue means trusted or proved, orange means untrusted, green means a result.
  Keep that meaning if you restyle them.
- Images `10`–`15` are not 16:9. Place them beside text.
